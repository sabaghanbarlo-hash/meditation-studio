Privacy Policy

Meditation Studio is a personal tool used by its owner to create meditation videos and upload them to the owner's own YouTube channel.

Data we access

The app uses the YouTube Data API only to upload videos, titles, descriptions, tags, and thumbnails to the channel of the Google account that authorized it. It does not read, store, or share any other Google account data.

Data storage

Authorization credentials are stored as encrypted secrets in the owner's private GitHub settings. Scripts and generated files are processed in GitHub Actions and are not sold or shared with third parties.

Third-party services

Use of YouTube is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
